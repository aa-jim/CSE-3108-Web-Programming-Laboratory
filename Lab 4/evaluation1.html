Classic Margherita Pizza

A timeless Italian recipe from Naples

Prep: 20min Cook: 12min Serve: 5min

About This Recipe

The classic Margherita Pizza is made with San Marzano tomatoes, white mozzarella cheese, and green basil leaves.

Ingredients

  • 500g bread flour (tipo 00)
  • 325ml warm water
  • 7g active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g San Marzano tomatoes
  • 250g fresh mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil for drizzling

Instructions

  1. 1 Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. 2 Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. 3 Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. 4 Arrange the mozzarella slices on top of the sauce.
  5. 5 Bake for 10-12 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
  6. 6 Remove from oven and top with fresh basil leaves.
  7. 7 Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with salt before serving.

Tip: For an extra burst of flavor, add a drizzle of balsamic glaze before serving.

Nutrition Per Serving
